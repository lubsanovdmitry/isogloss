# Data sources and licences

Derived tables in `engine/data/` are generated by `cmd/datagen` from:

| Source | Used for | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PHOIBLE 2.0 (Moran & McCloy), https://phoible.org | Segment features, segment frequencies | CC BY-SA 3.0 (derived tables are share-alike) |
| Concepticon, https://concepticon.clld.org | Concept IDs and glosses | CC BY 4.0 |
| CLICS³ (Rzymski et al.), https://clics.clld.org | Colexification edges (families ≥ 3) | CC BY 4.0 |
| WALS Online, https://wals.info | Word-order priors | CC BY 4.0 |
| IE-CoR (Heggarty et al.), https://iecor.clld.org | Romance word lists for intelligibility calibration | CC BY 4.0 |

Index Diachronica is used as a reference only; no data is copied from it.
Licences are to be re-confirmed at each regeneration (`make data`).

The real proto-language tables (`engine/data/protos/*.tsv`: Latin,
Proto-Indo-European, Proto-Germanic, Proto-Austronesian) are hand-written,
not generated: individual reconstructed or attested forms chosen from the
standard handbooks each file cites (Lewis & Short, de Vaan, Mallory &
Adams, Ringe, LIV², Kroonen, Blust & Trussel's ACD, Ross, Wolff), simplified
and given phonetic values by hand. No table is copied from those works.

Concept frequency classes, stability classes and taboo flags (`cmd/datagen/concepts_meta.tsv`) are hand-curated, informed by Pagel et al. (2007) and Holman et al. (2008).
